# Repository disclosures

## A. Purpose and educational scope

This repository is an educational networking primer and interview-preparation
resource. It is not a production runbook, security assessment, legal opinion,
vendor certification, or substitute for system-specific review.

Examples use fictional service names, reserved documentation addresses, local
fixtures, or intentionally simplified models. Results from a lab or interview
exercise do not establish behavior in a production environment.

## B. Standards, vendors, and version sensitivity

Protocol facts are identified with standards or primary references where
practical. F5, cloud-provider, Kubernetes, Linux, SDK, and other product
behavior may vary by release, license, configuration, CNI, provider, or local
policy. Vendor names and trademarks belong to their respective owners; this
repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by those vendors unless stated
otherwise.

Before relying on a claim, verify the deployed version, effective
configuration, supported feature set, and the relevant official documentation.
Engineering recommendations are inferences, not universal product guarantees.

## C. Safety, authorization, and privacy

Commands, automation examples, packet captures, and failure tests must be used
only against systems and data for which the operator has explicit
authorization. Prefer read-only inspection, local targets, reserved addresses,
bounded experiments, and reversible changes.

Do not place credentials, private keys, tokens, customer data, or unredacted
packet payloads in issues, commits, examples, or transcripts. Preserve incident
evidence and follow applicable retention, privacy, and security requirements.
